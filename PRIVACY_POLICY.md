# Privacy Policy

**Lifeline**
Last updated: November 11, 2026

## Overview

Lifeline respects your privacy.

This application:

* Does **not** collect any personal data
* Does **not** send any data to the internet
* Does **not** use analytics services
* Does **not** display advertisements

## Data Collection

Lifeline does collect, store and process on your local device information about applications and websites you have visited.

The app operates entirely offline and does not transmit any data outside your device.

## Permissions

The app only requests the minimum permissions required for its core functionality. It does not access:

* Contacts
* Location
* Camera
* Microphone
* Files or media
* Device identifiers

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party services, SDKs, tracking tools, or analytics providers.

## Children's Privacy

Since the app does not share any data, it is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes in the future, the updated version will be published on the app’s distribution page.
